# Included assets

`public/images/profile.jpg` is a fictional demo portrait created with the built-in image-generation tool. It does not depict Raj. It is 1122 × 1402 pixels, saved as a progressive JPEG. Replace it with your own photo and set `demoPhoto: false` in `src/profile.ts`.

Prompt: High-end black-and-white editorial studio portrait of a fictional Indian male developer in his mid twenties, with a natural realistic face that does not imitate any real person. Short textured hair, plain black crewneck, gentle confident relaxed expression, looking toward camera. Dark charcoal seamless background with subtle separation, cinematic soft key light and silver rim light, genuine skin texture. Vertical approximately 4:5 composition, full head and shoulders, body slightly angled, face upper-center, room below shoulders for CSS overlay. Tasteful and professional. No text, brand logos, graphics, UI, watermark, cut-off head, exaggerated retouching, or plastic skin.

The project previews are React interface illustrations in `src/project-previews.tsx`, styled in `src/index.css`. Icons are supplied by Lucide. The retained chrome artwork from the previous edition is available in `public/images/hero-chrome.webp`; this edition uses the portrait in the hero.
